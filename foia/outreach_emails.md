## 2. Office of the Chief Judge, Circuit Court of Cook County

**Subject:** Request for Pretrial Division judicial assignment records

Dear Office of the Chief Judge,

I'm a Cook County resident researching pretrial detention practices for a free, nonpartisan voter guide. I understand that the Illinois Freedom of Information Act doesn't apply to the courts. If your office has a process for public requests for administrative records, please treat this as one. If not, I'd be grateful to know whether these records can be released some other way.

I'm requesting records showing which judges were assigned to hear first appearances and pretrial detention hearings, by date, from September 18, 2023 to the present. This includes the Pretrial Division courtrooms at the Leighton Criminal Courthouse (including Courtroom 100 / Branch 1 and Courtroom 102), and the courtrooms in the municipal districts where detention hearings are held. Daily or weekly assignment schedules would be ideal. If those don't exist, I'd welcome any general administrative orders or rosters that assign judges to these courtrooms, along with their effective dates.

Electronic copies (spreadsheet or PDF) by email would be best. If there is a fee, please let me know the amount before processing. If part of this request can't be fulfilled, I'd appreciate whatever portion can be.

Thank you for your help.

[Your name]
[Mailing address]
[Email and phone]
